A maintenance routine for custom fishing rods

In brief

Give the owner a short after-trip and seasonal care routine tailored to the rod, its sections and its usual fishing conditions.

Provide a routine with the rod

Explain where to find the maker’s care directions and which parts of the custom handle or guide train deserve attention. Include rinsing after exposure to salt or grit and drying before the rod goes into its sleeve.

Add a seasonal check

At the end of the season, review the rod while it is clean and out of its case. Note changes at the guides, joints or handle, and keep the record with the rod. Make time for a workshop inspection if there are concerns about its condition.

Keep service details handy

Record the make, model and sections together with the builder’s contact details. If a guide or other component has been changed, add the replacement model and position to the service note so the next repair can refer back to it.
