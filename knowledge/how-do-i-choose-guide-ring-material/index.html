Choosing rod guide ring material

In brief

Compare guide inserts within the frame and size you need, and check the actual opening against your line and leader before ordering.

Start with the layout

Choose the guide’s job and required opening first. A tall reduction guide and a low running guide do different work. Picking the same insert material for both tells you nothing about whether either ring is in the right place.

Compare like for like

List the frame model, nominal size and ring material separately. For example, Fuji’s titanium KB chart lists Torzite and SiC versions. Compare the exact guide you plan to fit and ask for the clear opening if the drawing gives only the outside diameter. The material name alone does not establish knot clearance.
