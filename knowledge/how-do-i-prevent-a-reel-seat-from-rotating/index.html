Planning a reel seat that stays aligned

In brief

Check the fit at every seat support, mark the intended orientation and record the assembly product and its documented cure stage.

Check the complete dry assembly

Fit the seat, the intended reel and any supports called for by the seat design. Compare the seat bore and blank diameter at the actual mounting station. Use the specifications for the exact model. A dry seat that rocks on its supports needs its fit reviewed before bonding.

Give the seat a visible alignment reference

With the reel sitting in the planned orientation, mark a reference you can check again after removing the reel. Record the seat station and support arrangement. Flex Coat’s handle instructions treat seat location and shimming as separate checks in the assembly.

Keep the cure record with the fit record

Use the component and adhesive maker’s assembly instructions for the selected combination. Record the product and its documented service-ready stage as well as the seat position. If the seat body later turns with both hoods, the builder can compare the movement with this original assembly record.
