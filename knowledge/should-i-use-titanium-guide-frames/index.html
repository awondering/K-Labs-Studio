When to choose titanium rod guide frames

In brief

Compare titanium and steel frame options in the actual guide models and sizes you plan to put on the blank.

Separate frame and ring choices

Frame material is one line on the parts list and ring material is another. Fuji KB product tables, for example, list titanium frame options separately from ceramic ring options. Check the complete part number rather than inferring frame material from the insert name or finish colour.

Compare complete sets

For a weight comparison, weigh or obtain the mass of each exact guide model, including the top. Keep the intended guide heights and openings on the same sheet so that a lighter set with a different layout is easy to spot. Record which parts are near the tip separately from the total so you can see where a substitution changes the build.
