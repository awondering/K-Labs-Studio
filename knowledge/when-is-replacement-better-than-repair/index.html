When to replace a rod part instead of repairing it

In brief

Compare component repair, a matching replacement section and a full replacement using the actual damage, parts availability and quote.

Get an assessment at the right level

Start with the rod model, section and problem the customer reports. Ask whether a single fitted part, a complete section or the whole rod is being assessed. CTS and Tenkara Rod Co. offer different service routes, so check the maker before assuming the workshop is the only option.

Confirm that a section is an actual match

The replacement must be confirmed for the model and version. A similarly named current rod may use different section dimensions. Ask the supplier which part fits the remaining rod and whether it is available before promising the option.

Put practical choices side by side

Compare the quoted cost, delivery time and any parts that would be retained. For example, a matching section may preserve the handle and the other sections. If the maker has no matching part, record that fact before comparing a rebuild assessment with a replacement rod.
