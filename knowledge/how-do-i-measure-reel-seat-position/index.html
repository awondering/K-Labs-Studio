Measuring reel seat position on a rod blank

In brief

Use the finished butt and a named seat edge as your measurement references so the same handle layout can be fitted again.

Pick two physical points

One repeatable dimension runs from the finished butt to the rear face of the seat where the rear grip meets it. Fit the intended butt cap or fighting butt during the mock-up so both reference points are present.

Make the reference portable

Write the direction and units with the distance on the build sheet. If a customer supplies a favourite rod, use the same two reference points on the old and new handle. A number taken to the middle of the reel foot needs its own label.

Recheck before setting guide positions

Check the distance with the butt hardware and the seat in their finished dry-fit positions. In Studio Build Details, enter the distance from the butt and choose the corresponding rear, centre or front reference. Recheck the datum when either end of the measurement changes.
