Storing a multi-piece fishing rod

In brief

Dry the separated rod sections and use a sleeve and case that accommodate the longest section without crowding the tip.

Pack the rod dry

After a trip, take the rod down in accordance with the maker’s directions. Check that the sections and sleeve are dry before packing. If the sleeve got wet in transit, remove the sections and let the sleeve dry separately.

Give the tip room in its sleeve

Use the sleeve pockets where provided to separate the sections, and choose a case that fits the longest one. Check the ends before closing the case so that the tip and guides are clear of the closure.

Choose a suitable storage location

Keep the packed rod in a dry place away from direct sun and where the case will stay clear of other gear. Before the next trip, confirm every section is there and inspect the joints and guides while the rod is out of the sleeve.
