Spinning and casting guide trains compared

In brief

Identify the reel and line path before choosing guide heights or borrowing a spacing chart for a different rod type.

Start at the reel

A spinning reel sheds line in coils from a fixed spool. Its first guides manage those coils before the line reaches the running section. A casting reel sends line toward the stripper on a more direct path, so the tall reduction group used on many spinning rods solves a different problem.

Choose a chart for the build

If you have a casting blank and a spinning guide calculator, look for a casting reference that matches your components. Use the actual reel position and inspect the line path through the train. For both types, compare the running guide arrangement against the shape of the individual blank before making the positions permanent.
