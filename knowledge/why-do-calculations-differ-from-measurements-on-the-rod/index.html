Why calculated and measured figures differ

In brief

Rounded inputs, mixed units and different reference points make a correct calculation disagree with the rod. Check each before doubting the formula.

Inputs that are slightly off

A calculation is only as close as its inputs. A blank measured as 12.0 mm that is really 12.1 mm gives a circumference of 37.70 mm instead of 38.01 mm: 0.31 mm short.

Mixed units

A figure in inches read as millimetres is 25.4 times too small: 12 in is 304.8 mm, not 12 mm. Errors this large usually mean a unit mix-up.

Different reference points

Distances measured from opposite ends are complements: distance from the butt = rod length − distance from the tip. On a 2100 mm rod, guides 380 mm and 500 mm from the tip are 1720 mm and 1600 mm from the butt. Convert each position before comparing. A constant difference instead suggests checking for a displaced zero or a consistently different measurement point.

Check in this order

  1. Confirm both figures are in the same unit.
  2. Confirm both are measured from the same end and to the same point.
  3. Re-measure the inputs, then recalculate from the new figures.
