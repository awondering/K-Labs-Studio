Choosing a butt cap for a custom fishing rod

In brief

Match the cap to its mating surface and grip profile, then dry fit it to confirm the finished butt length and transition.

Check the type of fit

Find the mating surface, then compare its outside diameter with the bore of a cap that fits over it. Fuji’s BRC sheet lists the bore, outside profile and length separately.

Compare the assembled profile

Place the proposed cap against the rear grip in a dry fit. Look at the seam and feel the change in diameter where the parts meet. Two caps can fit a similar mating surface and still leave a different outline or finished handle length.

Use the finished butt as your reference

If you swap to a longer cap, recheck the distance from its finished end to the seat. Record the exact part number and material with the revised handle dimension so the same cap can be ordered again.
