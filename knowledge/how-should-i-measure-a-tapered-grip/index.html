Measuring a tapered fishing rod grip

In brief

Record the length, outside profile and bore of a tapered grip separately, with each diameter tied to its measurement station.

Label both ends

Identify the butt-facing and tip-facing ends. Measure the grip along its axis and record the outside size at each end. Where a shoulder or swell matters to the hand fit, mark that station from a named end and record its outside size there too.

Keep bore and outside profile separate

Use the caliper orientation and measuring faces intended for each dimension you are checking. The bore data describes fit on the blank. The outside sizes describe the shape and the join against the hardware. Record which reading is which when comparing cork and EVA versions of a similar grip.

Check the join in a dry fit

At the seat end, place the grip against the seat and compare the two shoulders. If a measurement seems off, return to the original station with the jaws square and steady. A reading farther along the taper describes a different part of the grip.
