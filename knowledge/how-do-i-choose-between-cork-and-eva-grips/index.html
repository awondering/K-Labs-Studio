Choosing between cork and EVA rod grips

In brief

Compare cork and EVA grips at the shape and size you will actually hold, including the bore and how each meets the reel seat.

Compare the finished shape

Compare cork and EVA in the handle shape and length you want to hold. American Tackle publishes separate cork and EVA foregrip listings. Specify the shape as well as the material so the two candidates are comparable.

Check the catalogue bore

Read the bore for the exact part. American Tackle’s cork table separates inside and outside diameters and length. Its EVA listing uses length and hole diameter in its sizing. Compare the part with the blank at the station where it will actually sit.

Dry fit the transition

Place the chosen foregrip at the seat and check it with the intended reel fitted. Look where the fingers cross the join and where the grip ends against the blank. Write down the material, part number and finished profile so a later reorder preserves the intended shape.
