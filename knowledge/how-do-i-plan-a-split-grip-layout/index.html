Planning a split-grip rod handle

In brief

Set the seat and hand positions first, then dry fit the rear grip, butt grip and checks to see where the exposed gap falls.

Start at the hand positions

Fit the intended reel and mock up the rear grip and butt grip at the positions the angler will use. Try the handle in the angler’s normal hold. A comfortable reference rod gives you a butt-to-seat dimension to transfer as a starting point.

Lay out the exposed gap

With the seat and grip pieces in place, measure the remaining exposed section between the two grip faces. If the gap is wrong for the intended handle, identify which part can move and check the handle feel again before fixing the layout.

Stage the checks in assembly order

Put each winding check against the face it will meet and label its station. Two checks on opposite ends of the gap can sit over different blank diameters. Include them in the dry assembly so the chosen parts and their order are recorded together.
