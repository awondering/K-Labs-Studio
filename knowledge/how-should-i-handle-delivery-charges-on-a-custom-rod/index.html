Quoting delivery charges for a custom rod

In brief

Record the agreed delivery method and customer charge in the quote, and keep the workshop’s freight and packaging assumptions traceable.

Price the delivery method you are offering

Confirm collection or courier and the destination before completing the quote. For courier, price the packed rod using the package you expect to send. Keep carrier assumptions with the job so a change in destination or packing can be checked against the original figure.

Check the customer-facing total

Studio’s Freight and Packaging fields are workshop cost inputs. Check the delivery charge and customer-facing total separately in the Customer Quote. If the customer changes to collection, check the revised fulfilment method and total before sending.

Keep a record when the charge changes

Record what the accepted quote included and what changed. If delivery was included in the price, say so in the quote. If it is priced separately, check that the separate line and final total agree before sending.
