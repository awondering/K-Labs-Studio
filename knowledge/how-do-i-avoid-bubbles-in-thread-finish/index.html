Identifying bubbles in rod thread finish

In brief

Distinguish air already in the mix from bubbles that appear at a guide foot, then check the finish maker’s process for the source you saw.

Observe when they first appear

Look at the mix before it goes on the rod. A cloud of small bubbles already in the cup points to a different stage from isolated bubbles that appear only near a foot after coating. Flex Coat documents both appearances.

Trace the mixing process

Compare the tools and mixing motion with the instructions for the product. Flex Coat distinguishes thorough mixing from whipping in air. Record the cup, stirrer and when you first saw froth rather than assuming the blank caused it.

Record the location on the rod

If the mix looked clear but bubbles occurred at the base of one frame, note the affected guide and wrap edge. A photo of that location and a record of the product are better starting points for the maker than a picture of the whole rod alone.
