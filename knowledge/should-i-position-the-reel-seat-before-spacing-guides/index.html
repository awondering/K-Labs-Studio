Setting reel-seat position before guide spacing

In brief

Establish the reel position before laying out the guide train, and record a repeatable reference for the assembled handle.

Mock up the handle first

Fit the intended reel in the proposed seat position before taking guide measurements. On a spinning build, moving the reel also moves the starting point for the reduction train. Record which part of the seat or reel foot you measured from so a second dry fit can reproduce it.

Recheck after a handle change

If the seat moves to make room for a longer rear grip, fit the reel again and revisit the path into the first guide before wrapping. Keep the old positions on the sketch until you have compared them with the new setup. This makes it easier to tell a genuine layout change from a shift in the measurement reference.
