Choosing a hook keeper for a fishing rod

In brief

Select a keeper that suits the rig and folds or sits where it clears the angler’s hand and the rest of the handle.

Match the keeper to the rig

Choose a keeper for the rig you want it to hold. For example, Fuji’s MHKM is an open-hook design with a retaining body. Its drawing also lists the range of rod diameters for each mounting ring.

Check the handle with the hardware in place

With the reel in place, check the keeper footprint where you plan to put it. For a ring-mounted keeper, measure the blank at that location, then check the maker’s fit range and clearance around your usual grip.

Record the keeper separately

Record the keeper model and its position separately from the line guides. Check the instructions for its mounting style before planning any wrap around it.
