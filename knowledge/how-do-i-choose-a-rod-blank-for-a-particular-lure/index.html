Choosing a rod blank for a particular lure

In brief

Start with the lure and rig you actually cast, then compare the blank maker’s rating, application, action and power for the exact model.

Write down the outfit

Record the common lure and its fishing configuration. If a soft plastic is normally fished on a jig head, weigh the combination and note the units. That gives you one repeatable comparison when reading the manufacturer’s model table.

Shortlist by application

Compare candidates listed for that style of fishing. A catalogue can show the same overall length on blanks with different action, power and lure ranges. If two candidates cover the everyday lure, compare those remaining specifications and the maker’s intended application before choosing the handle or guides.

Keep the model on the build sheet

Record the exact model and its listed range beside the actual rig you used to make the decision. If the customer changes from small jigs to larger swimbaits, revisit that shortlist with the new rig in hand.
