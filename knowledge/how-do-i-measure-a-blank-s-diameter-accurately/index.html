Measuring a fishing rod blank’s diameter

In brief

Use the caliper’s outside jaws at the actual component station, and repeat any reading that changes with the jaws or measuring position.

Set the station first

Mark where the seat, winding check or other part will sit. Measure the blank at that mark with the outside jaws. Write the position and units next to the diameter. A catalogue butt diameter gives a reference for the butt and does not describe every point along a taper.

Get a repeatable reading

Clean the measuring faces and check zero before starting. Keep the jaws square, use the area near the jaw root and apply steady contact force. If a reading changes, lift and reset the instrument at the same mark so you can check alignment before recording it.

Preserve the measurement context

If widths taken across different directions at the same station differ, record the orientation with each figure. For a rigid ring or seat, use the measurements to choose a candidate and dry fit the actual component at the mark.
