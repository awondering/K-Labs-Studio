Can a damaged rod guide insert be replaced?

In brief

Identify the guide frame and insert, then check whether the maker services that insert or supplies a complete guide assembly.

Identify the frame as well as the ring

Photograph the guide from the side and front, and record its position. Check the guide model and the part of the assembly that is damaged. Fuji identifies ring material and frame as separate product attributes. The nominal ring number alone does not establish an insert fit.

Ask what the maker supplies

Check the maker or repairer for an approved insert service for the exact frame. Fuji’s own guide history illustrates designs in which the ring and the surrounding frame were engineered together. If there is no documented insert service for the model, quote the complete compatible guide as the replacement option.

Keep the quote specific

Record whether the quote is for an insert service or the whole guide. For a complete guide, compare the frame footprint, height and line opening with what is on the rod. If the required part is a tip top, record the tube fit separately.
