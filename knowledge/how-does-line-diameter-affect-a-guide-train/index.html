How line diameter affects a guide train

In brief

Use the main line, leader and knot together when choosing guide openings or comparing two spinning setups.

Separate the two checks

In the reduction section, look at the path from the reel through the first guides. In the running section, check the bulkiest part of the rig that actually passes through it. A fine braid can be paired with a much bulkier leader connection, so main-line diameter alone leaves out an important part of the fit.

Record a comparable setup

If a customer swaps between two lines, list each line with its intended leader and knot. Keep the reel and guide layout fixed when comparing those two rigs. For example, if only the thicker leader catches in the runners, isolate the connection before changing the entire reduction train.
