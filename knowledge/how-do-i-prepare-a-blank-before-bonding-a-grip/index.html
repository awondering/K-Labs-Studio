Checking a blank before bonding a rod grip

In brief

Dry-fit the grip at its exact station and confirm the surface and adhesive instructions for the blank and grip materials before mixing.

Make the grip position repeatable

Mark the station and identify both grip ends. Dry fit the grip against its adjoining seat or check and record the bore fit you found. Flex Coat’s cork assembly example fits the cork to the taper before bonding. Keep this measurement separate from any decision about surface treatment.

Identify the surfaces involved

Record the blank model and finish and the actual grip material. Check the adhesive maker’s directions for the chosen surfaces, and the blank maker’s directions for its coating. WEST SYSTEM distinguishes surfaces by material in its own guidance, which illustrates why a general epoxy preparation recipe does not establish compatibility with a finished blank.

Keep the documented preparation with the job

Write down the product and the preparation approved for those parts before starting. If the instructions leave the blank coating or grip material unspecified, ask the maker rather than borrowing an abrasive grade or cleaning method from a different application.
