When a newly finished rod is ready to use

In brief

Use the maker’s service-ready guidance for the exact finish and assembly adhesive and record the conditions for each.

Identify which operations are still curing

List the finish on the guide wraps and any assembly adhesive used in the handle, including the time each was applied. The products can have different readiness stages. Flex Coat gives separate guidance for its assembly glue and wrap finish.

Read the stage that matches use

Use the maker’s documented stage for putting the product into service. A working time describes how long you can apply or handle the mix, and a recoat interval tells you when you may add another coat. Neither interval establishes that the rod is ready to fish.

Compare the actual conditions

Check the product’s specified conditions against the workshop record. If a coat was added later, record its own application time. Ask the maker when the rod can enter service if the documented conditions or stage do not match the job.
