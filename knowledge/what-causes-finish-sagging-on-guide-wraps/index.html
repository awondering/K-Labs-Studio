Why thread finish sags on a rod wrap

In brief

Locate the pooling or ridge and compare the film, support and rotation with the finish maker’s directions for that product.

Distinguish pooling from the shape below

A bead that develops during levelling is a different observation from a ridge visible at the same place in the dry wrap. Use side light and note when the shape first appeared. Compare a neighbouring wrap from the same mix.

Review the film and handling record

Record the product, coat sequence and how the rod was supported while the coat levelled. Flex Coat describes thinner and higher-build options and its own approach to even coverage. The support and rotation setting are worth recording when one wrap pools and others do not.

Use the maker’s remedy for the product

Before trying to flatten a cured edge or coating over it, determine whether the issue is a local run or a batch that has not cured as expected. Compare the recorded conditions with the maker’s guidance on the actual coating.
