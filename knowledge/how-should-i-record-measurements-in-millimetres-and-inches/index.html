Recording rod measurements in millimetres and inches

In brief

One inch is exactly 25.4 mm. Label every figure with its unit and the point it is measured from, and convert from the original measurement.

The exact conversion

Since 1959 the inch has been defined as exactly 25.4 mm, so converting between the two is exact arithmetic:

  • Millimetres = inches × 25.4
  • Inches = millimetres ÷ 25.4

Worked examples

  • A 7 ft rod is 84 in: 84 × 25.4 = 2133.6 mm.
  • A 300 mm grip: 300 ÷ 25.4 = 11.811 in.
  • Fractions convert exactly too: 1/8 in = 0.125 in = 3.175 mm, and 5/16 in = 0.3125 in = 7.9375 mm.

Convert once, from the original

Rounding before converting adds error. 300 mm rounded to 11.8 in converts back to 299.72 mm, 0.28 mm short. Keep the figure you measured and convert from that, rounding only the result.

Label the unit and the reference point

A length means nothing without the point it is measured from. On a 2133.6 mm rod, a position 300 mm from the tip is 1833.6 mm from the butt. Write the unit, the end it is measured from, and whether a fraction or a decimal was used beside every figure.

In Studio

Settings → Measurement Units offers Metric (mm), Decimal Inches and Fractional Inches. Guide Setup lists guide positions from the tip; a build's reel seat position is recorded from the butt.
