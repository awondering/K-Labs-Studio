Why a reel loosens in its seat

In brief

Check whether the reel foot moves inside the hoods, a nut backs off, or the entire seat body moves relative to the handle.

Locate the movement without the line pulling

With the rod at rest, watch the foot and both hoods while checking where the movement appears. Look separately at the nut and the barrel relative to the grip. A moving reel foot and a moving seat body identify different fit questions for the workshop.

Check the design and the reel together

Compare the actual reel foot with the installed seat and note which end is loose. Fuji’s DPS drawings distinguish the screw hood and the separate locknut on some models. Identify the hardware on your seat before expecting it to work like another model.

Hand the observation to the builder

For example, report whether the rear hood lost its position, or whether both hoods stayed with the reel while the seat body moved. Add the reel model and a photo of the assembled fit. That helps the builder assess the relevant interface rather than treating every movement as a loose nut.
