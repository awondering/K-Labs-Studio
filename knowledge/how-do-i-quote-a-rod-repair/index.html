Preparing a quote for a rod repair

In brief

Describe the repair you are pricing, the parts included and how you will handle additional work found during the job.

Quote a stated scope

Refer to the intake record and name the part and position, such as replacing the stripper and matching its wrap colour as closely as stock allows. State whether the quote includes parts, labour and return delivery, and identify anything still awaiting inspection.

Price the unknowns separately

If several guide options are possible, write down which one the amount covers. If the condition of the surrounding area could change the scope, give an inspection step and agree when to contact the owner with a revised price.

Send a version the owner can accept

Identify the rod, scope, customer total and expected timing in the version you send. Keep the accepted version with the job. If the customer later asks for a complete guide set, describe and price that as a new scope.

Business.govt.nz, How to prepare a quote (https://www.business.govt.nz/browse-our-resource-library/tips-for-tradies/how-to-prepare-a-quote)
