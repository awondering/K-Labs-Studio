Comparing rod guide sets by total weight

In brief

Weigh the complete guides and tip top for each candidate layout, and record where the pieces that account for the difference sit.

Compare the same inventory

Set out the exact guides in order from stripper to tip. Tare a tray and weigh each complete set with its intended top. Make a note of which pieces you counted. Quoted weights for a single guide and a full set answer different purchasing questions.

Keep the positions beside the weights

Write down the frame, ring and station for the pieces changed between sets. For example, a lighter tip and a heavier stripper could give close total weights while changing the parts fitted at opposite ends of the rod. The total alone leaves that difference hidden.

Carry the clearance check forward

Keep the candidate set’s clear openings on the bench sheet alongside the measured weights. If the lighter option changes the smallest opening, check the line and leader connection before settling the parts list.
