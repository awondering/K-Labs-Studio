Cleaning a fishing rod after saltwater use

In brief

Rinse the rod with fresh water, attend to the guides and grip, then wipe it down and let it dry before storage.

Rinse the areas that collect grit and salt

Use fresh water on the blank, guide frames, rings and the grip area. Work along the rod so the undersides of the guides get attention too. For periodic cleaning, follow the rod maker’s instructions for any cleaner and use a soft cloth.

Dry before packing

Wipe off the water with a clean cloth and give the rod time to air dry. Check around the frame feet and handle seams before slipping it into its sleeve. Deal with the reel separately under its own maintenance instructions.

Take a second look at stubborn marks

Once the rod is clean, inspect the spot again. If it still looks chipped or corroded, record which guide or fitting it is and arrange an inspection. Keep suspected component damage separate from the cleaning job.
