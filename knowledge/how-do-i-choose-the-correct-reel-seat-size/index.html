Choosing the correct reel seat size

In brief

Check how the seat fits the blank, how its hoods capture the reel foot, and how the outside profile joins the grips.

Measure the blank at the seat

Measure at the planned seat position. Compare the bare blank or the proposed arbor with the seat’s specified bore, depending on its design. The Fuji DPS table has separate columns for catalogue size and inside diameter.

Fit the actual reel

Seat the reel foot under both hoods and close the locking mechanism during the dry fit. Check which parts of the reel foot sit inside the hoods and whether there is usable adjustment left. Repeat with any alternate reel the customer expects to fish.

Check the outside of the assembly

Compare the seat shoulders with the adjacent grip profiles. If a larger seat barrel solves a bore problem, fit the grips again and check the shape in the hand before ordering the full handle set.
