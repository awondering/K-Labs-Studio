What to include on a rod specification sheet

In brief

Record the exact blank and fitted components, named measurement references and model-specific ratings so the finished layout can be understood later.

Identify the rod before listing dimensions

Record the maker and model of the blank, the finished length and the type of build. Label line and lure ratings separately and keep their published units. Distinguish the maker’s rating from a finished measurement taken at the bench.

Make the layout repeatable

Record the reel seat position and its reference point, the guide order, and the exact parts and sizes where known. State whether guide positions run from the tip or butt so another builder can interpret the sheet correctly.

Separate work notes from customer details

Use the workshop record for measurement references, substitutions and other details needed to reproduce the layout. Review the customer document for the finished identity and agreed parts. Studio’s customer documents keep internal costs and margins out of the customer view.
