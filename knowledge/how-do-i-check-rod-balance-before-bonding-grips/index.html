Checking rod balance during a handle dry fit

In brief

Compare handle positions with the intended reel fitted and the grip and butt pieces in place before committing to the layout.

Assemble the parts you are comparing

Fit the intended reel and temporarily assemble the seat and handle. Compare the positions you would actually fish. An unfinished mock-up gives a preliminary comparison, so record any parts that are still missing.

Compare from the fishing grip

When comparing seat positions, use the same reel and hold the outfit from the same point on the proposed grip. Note whether the tip still wants to fall. Changing your grip point changes the leverage of the rest of the outfit.

Keep the preferred setting

Record the butt-to-seat dimension, the hand position and the fitted parts. If the reel or butt hardware changes later, repeat the comparison with the new part before using the old measurements as the final layout.
