Why clear thread finish can look cloudy

In brief

Note whether cloudiness appears in the mixing cup or only on the rod, then compare the condition with the coating maker’s guidance.

Locate the haze in the process

Flex Coat describes appearance changes while its finish is being mixed. That transient appearance is different from a haze that persists in a coating after the maker’s mix procedure or appears later on the rod. Record which stage you saw.

Describe the pattern in the film

Note whether the area is a uniform haze, a collection of fine bubbles or a patch that follows one wrap. WEST SYSTEM lists condensation and entrained air for some clear epoxy coatings. Treat those as examples of mechanisms rather than proof that one explains a particular rod finish.

Record the surroundings and the mix

Keep the product, application time, conditions and observations for the same batch together. If the manufacturer is assessing the haze, include whether a sample from that mix behaved the same way. Leave the cause open when those observations do not distinguish it.
