A rod quote versus a completed build record

In brief

Use the quote to record the proposed scope and price, and the completed record to document the rod and components actually delivered.

Keep the proposed build identifiable

A quote sets out the work and price for the customer to consider. Keep the version the customer accepted with the job reference. If the specification later changes, retain the original and record the agreed change against it.

Record the built rod at handover

Record the finished specification and components actually fitted. If a different guide was agreed because a part was unavailable, the completed record should identify the fitted model while the accepted quote still shows the initial plan.

Use the document for its stage

Studio’s document progression for a rod build is Customer Quote, Build Confirmation and Build Summary. Check the current stage before sending a document, and use the Customer Invoice when issuing the charge.
