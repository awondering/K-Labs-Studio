Documenting a rod repair at intake

In brief

Record the rod’s identity, its visible condition and the customer’s account before preparing a repair estimate or accepting work.

Identify what arrived

Record the make, model and serial number when they are legible. Take an overall photo and a close-up with a location reference, such as the second running guide from the tip. For a multi-piece rod, label which section is pictured and note which sections and accessories came in.

Separate observation from history

For example, write “insert missing from second runner” in the condition note and keep “owner noticed this after transport” in the customer history. That gives the workshop a useful description without turning an account of events into a diagnosis.

Hand off a clear next step

Use the intake record to agree which part is being assessed and whether the customer wants an estimate first. Keep the photos and the date with the job so the estimate and finished record refer to the same rod.
