Why a guide foot shows through a thread wrap

In brief

Distinguish a visible silhouette beneath translucent thread from exposed metal or a ridge where the wrap crosses the guide foot.

Look from two directions

View the wrap straight on for a change in colour, then from the side for a raised line or lifted turns. A visible silhouette under a continuous thread layer is a different observation from a gap that exposes metal.

Check the chosen thread and finish

Compare a sample using the intended thread and background. Fuji, for example, offers opaque and transparent EZ thread variants. A visible silhouette under a continuous wrap can be an appearance choice rather than evidence of exposed metal.

Isolate the point where coverage changes

If the problem is raised thread or a gap, mark the part of the foot where it starts. Review the dry wrap and compare how the turns meet at the transition. The manufacturer’s wrap instructions for the chosen thread and frame guide that assessment.
