Planning a spinning rod’s guide spacing

In brief

Start from the spool and reduction system, then lay out the transition and running section as separate parts of the same guide train.

Select the right spinning reference

Fit the intended reel and record the spool reference. Fuji’s published guide notes describe different layouts for its New Guide Concept and KR Concept. Use the system that matches the guide family and reel arrangement you are planning rather than mixing positions from different charts.

Lay out the whole reduction group

Set the first guide and transition on the sketch, then compare the positions and heights between them. If the entry guide changes, check the view from the spool through the intermediate rings again. A spacing list without the intended guide models leaves out the frame heights that shape the path.

Record the running section independently

Keep the transition and the low runners identified on the build drawing. Record their stations from one named reference and update the drawing when a guide moves. Studio’s Guide Setup distinguishes guide positions from the gaps between adjacent guides.
