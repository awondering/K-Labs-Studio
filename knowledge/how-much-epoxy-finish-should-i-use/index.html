Planning how much rod wrap finish you need

In brief

Plan the mix around the product’s measuring limits and the actual wraps, keeping mixed quantity separate from built-up film thickness.

Count the areas that will be coated

List the guide wraps, trim and decorative areas that you plan to coat. A tall frame with long wraps presents a different job from a few short runners. Use any kit coverage estimate as a buying guide rather than a specification for each wrap.

Plan the measuring setup

Check the exact finish instructions for the measuring tools and minimum batch they describe. Flex Coat gives separate recommendations for its measuring methods. A small number of wraps does not in itself justify changing the ratio or guessing at a smaller measurable batch.

Record the film you are aiming for

A quantity prepared in the cup and the amount on each wrap are different measurements. If you want a lighter coat, compare the coating options and manufacturer’s instructions for the intended build. Keep the product and coat count with the job notes.
