Why rod thread finish can remain tacky

In brief

Compare the exact product, measuring and mixing record, and elapsed cure conditions before assuming a tacky wrap needs another coat.

Separate time from a batch problem

Check how long the coat has been in the recorded conditions and compare that with the product instructions. If several wraps from one mix remain tacky while wraps from another mix have cured, retain the batch distinction.

Check the resin and hardener record

Flex Coat names measurement errors and incomplete mixing as possible reasons for its finish staying sticky. Compare the resin, hardener and measuring method with its instructions. If the quantities were not recorded, list that as missing information rather than diagnosing the ratio from touch alone.

Use a product-specific disposition

Different manufacturers give different rework instructions for different degrees of incomplete cure. Contact the finish maker with the batch and condition details before choosing how to deal with the coating. The same symptom across products does not establish the same remedy.
