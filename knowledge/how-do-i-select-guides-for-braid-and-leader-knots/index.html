Choosing guides for braid and leader knots

In brief

Size the running guides and tip top for the actual tied leader connection, including any knot that sits on the reel before the cast.

Check the assembled connection

Tie the connection you intend to fish using the actual line and leader. Check it through the smallest running ring and tip top in both directions. Keep leader length in the test setup. A knot that remains outside the tip has a different route from one wound through the guide train before casting.

Find the bottleneck

If the knot clears every runner but snags at the tip, inspect the top separately. Compare the clear opening and entry shape instead of ordering by nominal guide number alone. A useful bench record names the knot and leader and identifies the first guide where you feel a catch.
