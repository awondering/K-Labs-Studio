Why line touches the blank on a spiral rod

In brief

Locate the specific span where the line touches the blank, then compare the guide geometry with the spiral method used.

Name the span and condition

Record the guides on either side of the contact and whether it showed up at rest or in an existing static check. Mark their stations and rotations on the sketch. Contact near the first underside runner differs from contact between two underside runners.

Read the spiral design before changing spacing

In Kirkman’s Simple Spiral, the optional side guide is for clearance between the top and underside portions. It serves a different role from an underside runner. A stepped spiral can allocate those jobs differently, so identify the specific plan used before borrowing a correction.

Recheck the actual layout

Compare the installed guide height, position and rotation in the affected span with the layout record. Any assessment of a revised assembly should follow the blank and guide maker’s validation guidance. Record whether the contact persisted instead of assuming one change solved it.
