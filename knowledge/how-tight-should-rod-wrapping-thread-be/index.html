Setting rod-wrapping thread tension

In brief

Set up a steady feed and use a dry sample to judge whether the turns stay in place through the changes in the wrap profile.

Start with repeatable feed

Route the thread through the wrapper as its maker shows, and check that it feeds steadily. Fuji’s hand wrapper includes a tensioner. Record the setting on your wrapper if you need to repeat the comparison with the same spool.

Judge the wrap on a sample

Lay several turns and compare how they sit next to one another as the profile changes. The chosen thread can affect how readily a turn slips. Fuji describes that behaviour for its EZ thread. Compare the sample at a known setting rather than using one tension number for every material.

Identify what moved

If the turns stop tracking evenly, note whether the thread feed changed, the blank shifted in its supports, or the frame moved during the dry wrap. Restore a consistent reference and compare again with one setting changed at a time.
