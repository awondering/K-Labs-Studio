Where checks and trim rings fit on a rod handle

In brief

Identify the mating surfaces for each ring, then use its drawing to match the blank, grip or seat shoulder in the dry assembly.

Look at the drawing before the finish colour

A ring at a grip-to-blank transition must fit that blank station. A trim ring at the front of a seat can register against a different shoulder. Fuji gives separate drawings for WCH foregrip fittings and WCP seat-front rings, with more than one relevant diameter on some models.

Measure the two pieces that meet it

Identify what passes through the ring and what seats against its edge or recess. Compare those dimensions with the part drawing. If two rings share a nominal size, check both their bore and outside profile before treating them as interchangeable.

Put it on the assembly sketch

Label each ring by station and part number, for example seat front or tip-side end of the foregrip. Check the widths with the grips and seat together. A different trim width can shift the shoulder used to measure the handle.
