What a static guide check tells you

In brief

A static guide check compares the line path with the bent blank. A calculated spacing gives starting positions for that physical assessment.

Read two shapes at once

In a static guide check, the builder observes the bend of the actual blank and the segments of line between guides. The record can show a span with line contact or a sharp departure from the guide next to it. Fuji lists the bend and line path as separate checks in its guide reference.

Use the spacing plan as the starting point

A chart or calculation supplies stations from its inputs. It has no observation of the actual blank, fitted frame heights or finished line path. Compare the plan with the physical assessment specified for the blank and guide system. Keep a calculation on the build sheet as a proposal until the physical observations are recorded.

Keep static and casting results separate

A static check reports what happened in a stationary condition. An on-water cast also involves the lure, reel and moving line. Keep the two results separate so the reader knows whether a note refers to the bend or to observed casting behaviour.

Fuji Kogyo, 2023 reference guide specifications (https://www.fujitackle.com/cms-fuji/wp-content/uploads/2023/04/01_intro.pdf)
