Inspecting a new fishing rod blank at intake

In brief

Confirm the blank against its model specifications and document any visible discrepancy before fitting custom components.

Match what arrived to the order

Read the model label, count the sections and compare the supplied blank with the maker’s specifications. Keep model, length, butt dimension and tip designation separate. If a dimension differs, confirm the units and where it was taken before asking the supplier to identify the blank.

Create a visual record before assembly

Look over each section in good light and keep separate notes for the surface, ends and joint areas. Photograph an apparent discrepancy with a label or named end in view. Describe what you can see, for example a mark beside the joint, without claiming you know its cause from appearance alone.

Keep any supplier decision with the job

If there is a question about the identity or condition, send the model number, photos and measurement reference to the supplier. Keep the response with the build record before fitting custom parts. CTS, for instance, addresses material and workmanship defects separately from modification in its service guidance.
