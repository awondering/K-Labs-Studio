Choosing a left or right spiral guide layout

In brief

Choose the transition side for the reel, holder and handling arrangement, and put the viewing direction on the drawing.

Define left from a fixed viewpoint

Use a named viewing direction, such as looking from the butt toward the tip. Mark the top of the reel seat as the common reference. That way a side written on a bench sketch means the same thing when the rod is turned around.

Look at the rod in its normal resting position

With the intended reel fitted, compare the side clearances in the holder or rack the angler uses. Kirkman gives an example in which a side guide is placed with deck storage in mind. Treat reel-handle position and storage clearance as inputs for the particular build.

Write down the reason for the choice

If either side will accommodate the planned route around the blank, record which side was selected and why. Another angler with a different holder or reel can then see whether to keep your orientation or mirror the drawing.
