Estimating bench time for a rod repair

In brief

Break the job into inspection, parts matching and bench work. Keep hands-on time separate from the collection date.

Price the bench time in stages

List what has to be checked, what has to be matched or ordered, and the work you can already see. If the repair is an isolated guide replacement, list the bench operations for that guide separately from work elsewhere on the rod. Estimate the known stages before allowing for uncertain ones.

Keep elapsed time separate

A short bench operation can still have a later handover if the matching part is unavailable. Record parts availability and the current bench queue alongside the estimate. Give the customer an update point if either remains uncertain.

Review the estimate when the scope changes

If a check identifies another damaged guide, keep the original estimate on the job and prepare the additional scope on its own line. On completion, compare the time spent with the estimate to improve the next similar quote.
