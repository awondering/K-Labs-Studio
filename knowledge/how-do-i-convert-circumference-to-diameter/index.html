How to convert between diameter and circumference

In brief

Circumference is diameter multiplied by pi, and diameter is circumference divided by pi. Worked examples, rounding, and shapes that are not round.

The formulas

Pi (π) is the ratio of any circle's circumference to its diameter, about 3.14159. So:

  • Circumference = π × diameter
  • Diameter = circumference ÷ π

Use the same unit for both. The answer is in the unit you measured in: millimetres in, millimetres out.

Worked examples

  • A blank 12.0 mm in diameter: 12.0 × π = 37.70 mm around.
  • A grip that measures 100 mm around: 100 ÷ π = 31.83 mm in diameter.
  • A blank 0.500 in in diameter: 0.500 × π = 1.571 in around.

Rounding

Keep π to at least 3.1416 and round only the final answer. Using 3.14 puts every result about 0.05% out, which is 0.02 mm on the circumference of a 12 mm blank.

Shapes that are not round

The formulas describe circles only. For an oval or irregular section, circumference ÷ π gives the diameter of a circle with the same distance around, not the section's actual width in any direction.
