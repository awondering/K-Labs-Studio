Where a spiral guide transition finishes

In brief

Identify the first guide in the underside run and record its station separately from any side guide used to keep line clear of the blank.

Identify the end by orientation

On the bench plan, mark the first guide that sits in line with the underside runners. That is where the line path joins the underside run. Record its reference and station. Its position cannot be inferred from the rod length alone.

Label the parts on either side

A stepped spiral can use rotating guides before reaching the underside. In Kirkman’s Simple Spiral, the optional side guide has a clearance role, while the first underside runner still belongs to the main run. Keep those parts separate on the drawing.

Review the entry into the underside run

Read the path from the preceding guide to that first underside frame and then to the next runner. If a frame or rotation changes, revisit those two segments on the drawing and on the actual assembly. Record the chosen end of the transition instead of applying an angle or distance from a different method.
