What a winding check does on a fishing rod

In brief

A winding check finishes the transition around the blank at a grip or seat. Size each one where its finished face will sit.

Find the transition

A check is a ring that sits at a handle transition, commonly where the foregrip ends on the blank or where a split grip meets the exposed section. Lay it out against the adjoining face so you can see which end and profile need to match.

Size each station separately

Mark the intended contact point and measure the blank there. On a tapered split-grip blank, the forward and rear checks can require different bores. Label the two parts by position before taking the handle apart.

Dry fit in sequence

Stage the checks with their neighbouring grip parts and run through the order they go on the blank. Look down the assembly for an obvious shoulder or gap before choosing the outside profile of each check.
