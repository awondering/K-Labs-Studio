What rod power and action describe

In brief

Power describes resistance to bending. Action describes where the bend develops, so keep both labels when comparing blank models.

Read power and action separately

Power describes how much the rod resists bending. Action describes the shape of that bend. Faster actions concentrate more of the initial flex toward the tip, while a moderate action typically bends farther down. A rod can have substantial power and still have a moderate action.

Compare within the intended use

Lamiglas gives different examples of what medium power means for surf and steelhead rods. Read the model’s intended application and stated line and lure ratings alongside its power label. Record the manufacturer’s wording rather than converting one brand’s category into another.

Write both fields on the order

If a customer asks for a medium rod, establish which rod family they have in mind and ask what sort of bend they like. Keep the requested action separate on the order sheet so that a change of blank model does not quietly change both choices.
