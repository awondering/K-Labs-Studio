What an offset stripper guide does

In brief

An offset stripper is the first guide rotated away from the reel-up reference. Its position and the line approach must be considered together.

Identify which guide is offset

The stripper is the first guide after the reel. In some spiral designs this guide is rotated around the blank, so its ring centre sits away from the reel-up line. A small side guide farther along the train is another component. Check the drawing before calling both guides a stripper.

Draw the approach from the reel

Record the angle of the first guide as well as its distance from the line exit. Inspect the path from the fitted reel to that guide and into the next one. Moving the first ring around the blank changes the approach from the reel, so compare the path with the reel you intend to use.

Keep the model choice explicit

Kirkman’s Simple Spiral keeps the first guide on top, while the published offset example uses a rotated first guide. If you are following someone else’s layout, record which arrangement you copied and distinguish its entry-guide angle from any general rotation labels in your bench notes.
