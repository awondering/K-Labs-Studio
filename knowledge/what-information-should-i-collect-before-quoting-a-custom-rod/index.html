What to ask before quoting a custom rod

In brief

Gather the intended fishing setup, fit preferences, scope and delivery details before pricing the custom build.

Ask how the rod will be fished

Get the technique, intended reel and line arrangement, and the lure or rig the customer normally casts. Ask what they want to keep or change from a rod they already use. A specific reel and familiar handle give the builder a better brief than a finish colour alone.

Separate decisions from options

Write down the agreed blank or intended specification, handle arrangement and any essential components. Put undecided items on a list, with alternatives if useful. If a component choice can materially change cost, keep it visible before sending a price.

Confirm the commercial details

Record the customer details, estimated timing, delivery method and any deposit arrangement to include in the quote. Read back the key scope and assumptions before treating the document as ready for the customer.
