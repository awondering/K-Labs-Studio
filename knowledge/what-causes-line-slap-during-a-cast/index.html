What line slap looks like during a cast

In brief

Line slap is contact between moving line and the blank. Identify where contact occurs before comparing guide or reel changes.

Locate the contact

On a spinning rod, look for contact between the outgoing line and the blank near the reduction guides. Fuji’s KR notes identify line slap as one reason for using a tall entry frame in that system. Noise alone cannot identify where contact occurred.

Keep the incident description specific

Note whether the issue appears on line release or later in the cast. Include the spool, line and leader, and mark the suspected span between guides. If you cannot see contact, describe the sound or tangle and leave line slap as a possibility.

Compare one change at a time

If you compare setups, keep the rod and lure the same while checking the line or spool combination. Compare the guide frame positions with the intended reduction layout before replacing parts. Fuji treats the entry and reduction group as a system, so an isolated ring size does not describe the complete path.
