Diagnosing a break in fishing line

In brief

Keep the broken line and inspect the end, nearby braid and guide path before deciding whether the break began at a knot or a contact point.

Preserve what you can inspect

Lay the remaining pieces out in order and note where the leader connection was relative to the visible break. Take a photo before discarding anything. Mark whether nearby line is fuzzy, flattened or nicked, all signs Shimano’s PowerPro care guide includes in its braid inspection.

Trace the surfaces the line touched

Inspect the relevant guide openings and any known contact points outside the rod. For example, if the angler saw the line pass a dock edge, record that alongside any mark found in a guide. Finding a rough-looking point is a reason to inspect it, rather than proof that it caused the break.

Record the result you can support

Keep the line type, leader, knot, where the break occurred and what you found in the guide train together. If the break point is missing, say so. A repairer can then compare the evidence with the rod without starting from an assumed cause.
