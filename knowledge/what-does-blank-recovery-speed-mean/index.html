What rod blank recovery speed describes

In brief

Recovery describes how a blank settles after it has been deflected. Keep it separate from the action label and the finished rod’s balance.

Describe the motion you see

After the blank unloads, its tip can continue to move before settling. Recovery is about that response over time. The action label describes how the rod bends. Scott’s ReAct explanation treats residual vibration and recovery as design considerations alongside stiffness.

Compare the same state of assembly

When comparing descriptions, check what was assessed: a bare blank or a finished rod. Record the guide set and other fitted parts when taking workshop notes. If one rod is bare and the other is finished, label that difference before attributing what you see to the blank design.

Use a specific note instead of a rating

Write down whether you mean the initial return toward straight or the movement that continues afterwards. If a manufacturer does not publish a measurement method, keep your observation as a qualitative comparison and avoid assigning a time or performance ranking from the action label.
