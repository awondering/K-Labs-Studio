What a spinning guide choke point does

In brief

Locate the transition from the reduction group to the running guides and check how the guides on either side work together.

Identify the transition

The choke point marks where the reduction train hands the line over to the running section. In some Fuji KR examples a running-size KB belly guide sits at that handoff. Fuji’s KR guide notes also show a layout with a separate low third reduction guide before the belly guide, so check the chart for the layout you are using.

Check the neighbouring guides

Review the approach from the last reduction guide and the exit toward the tip. If you move the transition in a mock-up, keep track of the two adjacent gaps and compare the whole path again. A layout calculator can propose a starting position when its guide series and rod type match your build.
