Why a fishing rod can bend unevenly

In brief

Compare the observed curve with the intended action and recorded guide layout. A local change needs its own observation and assessment.

Name what you observed

Some action profiles concentrate more bend near the top, and some spread it farther down the blank. Identify whether the observation is a broad bend profile or a local change at one station. Record the rod and section instead of concluding from the word uneven alone.

Compare against the same setup

Use the original layout or check record for that rod, including joint positions, guide stations and the conditions of the observation. Fuji describes looking at the bend and line path together. If a guide or joint differs from the previous record, note the difference before assigning a cause.

Record a new or unexplained change separately

A change observed after an impact is a separate question about condition. Preserve the photos and the history for an assessment. A comparison of the bend is useful information for the maker, not a finding about the condition of the blank wall.
