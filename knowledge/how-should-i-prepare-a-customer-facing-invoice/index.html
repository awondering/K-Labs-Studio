Preparing a customer invoice for a custom rod

In brief

Check the customer, rod description, charges and payment details before issuing the invoice, then retain the issued document.

Identify the work and the parties

Check the business and customer details, invoice reference, issue date and due date. Describe the rod or repair in terms the customer recognises. If the order contains more than one rod, use the build references or descriptions to make the lines easy to match.

Review the amounts before issue

Check the accepted scope, charges and currency against the document. For New Zealand GST records, consult Inland Revenue’s taxable supply information guidance for the type and value of supply. A workshop checklist and the applicable tax-record requirements serve different purposes.

Keep the issued version

In Studio, preview the invoice before issue. Issuing saves a customer-facing snapshot. Use the existing invoice for a reprint and the Payment Statement for later payments, so the customer can see which document records the charge and which reflects payment activity.
