Choosing guide frames for a spiral-wrapped rod

In brief

Select guide frames by their job at the reel entry, around the blank and along the underside running section.

Choose the entry frame for the fitted reel

Compare the guide near the reel with the line exit and proposed route into the wrap. Write down the frame, height and clear opening. A familiar ring number alone does not tell you where the centre of that opening will sit.

Specify a side guide by its purpose

Some spiral designs place guides around the side as part of a gradual transition. Kirkman’s Simple Spiral uses a different division of jobs: its optional low side guide exists to keep line off the blank. Identify which design you are building before selecting that piece.

Plan the underside parts as a group

List the running guides and top, and compare the openings to the actual rig. In Kirkman’s method the underside runners are matched in size. Record that as a choice for that particular method rather than borrowing only one piece of the design for an otherwise different layout.
