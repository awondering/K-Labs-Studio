Recording components on a rod refurbishment

In brief

Record what was retained, supplied and replaced, with each component tied to its position and the finished job.

Separate parts by outcome

On the bench record, identify each item and mark whether it stayed on the rod or was replaced. For instance, distinguish the existing stripper that was retained from the replacement runner and top supplied for the job. Keep their positions clear if several look alike.

Record the fitted item

For a replacement, record the exact model, size and quantity where known. When using a Studio library item, select the specific component and size so the record reflects the part used. Keep custom or unidentified parts clear in the workshop notes.

Review the record at handover

Compare planned replacements with what was actually fitted. Check the customer’s description against the finished rod and keep internal cost details on the workshop side of the record.
