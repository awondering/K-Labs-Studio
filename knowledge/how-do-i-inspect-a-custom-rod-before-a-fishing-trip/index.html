Inspecting a custom rod before a fishing trip

In brief

Look over the blank, guides, ferrules and handle before packing, and note exactly where any change from the last trip appears.

Start with the sections out of the sleeve

Lay out the sections in order and inspect the blank and the outside of the joints. Work along the guide train in sequence. Examine the ring and frame under good light, then check that all the sections and any removable handle parts are accounted for.

Record anything that has changed

If a mark remains after cleaning, describe its position and take a photo. For example, note whether the mark is in the ring, on the frame, or on the wrap beside the foot. A close-up and a wider photo make it easier for a builder to identify the component.

Make the check repeatable

Use the same inspection order next time. Keep a note of items sent for inspection with the rod and the date, so a later bench visit starts with a useful description of when the change was first seen.
