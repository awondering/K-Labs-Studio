Why fishing rod thread wraps develop gaps

In brief

Find where adjacent turns first separate, then compare thread feed and placement before changing thread or adding finish.

Inspect the turns before coating

Locate the first gap and note whether the separation is in the middle of a plain wrap or where the surface changes at a foot. A repeatable location gives you a better starting point than a description of the whole wrap as loose.

Check feed and the supports

See whether the spool feeds at the same tension when you repeat the wrap on a sample. Check that the rod is supported in the same position. Fuji’s thread notes recognise that slip and turn placement are affected by the thread used, while the wrapper controls the way it feeds.

Record the change that helped

Compare the sample after changing one setting. If steady feed closes gaps on the plain section but the problem returns at a frame edge, log those as separate findings. That avoids changing the wrap recipe for portions that already sit evenly.
